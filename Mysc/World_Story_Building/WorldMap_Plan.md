## Lurian Common Tongue

Derived from Myriac very long ago (like Indo-European → German, but even more distant). Used by the people of Lura for everyday names — roads, newer settlements, trade terms. Ancient geographic features (mountains, rivers, forests) still carry their Myriac names; features built or named by people in more recent history use Lurian.

**Sound character:** Japanese-influenced open syllables. Myriac roots are faintly traceable by a linguist who knows both languages and the shift rules — but you cannot guess the Myriac word just from hearing the Lurian one.

### Phonetic shift rules (Myriac → Lurian)

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Medial/final **t → n** | Mito → Mino |
| **p → h** (bilabial weakening) | Paruk → Haru |
| **Cy / hy clusters → sh** (palatalization; non-nasal C) | Hyro → Shiro, Chaxo → Kashi |
| **my → n** (nasal-palatal collapse; vowels shift fully) | Myra → Niru |
| **v → b** or drops | Yaves → Yabi |
| **x → sh** | Xuton → Shudo |
| **t → d** (voicing, medial) | Gaton → Gado |
| Consonant clusters break up with inserted vowels | Bres → Buri |
| Final stops and -uk endings drop | Paruk → Har-, Welc → Wer- |
| Long compounds shorten — syllables can vanish | — |

## The Continent — Overview

The continent has one great river (**Trevelc** [tre-felk]) that originates in Lura's western mountains, flows through the valley it carved in Lura's western range, then continues south through Tyriova along the Tyriova/Gavonchaxo border before emptying into the southern oceans. This river valley is the continent's primary overland trade route and the only well-traversable exit from Lura.

### Continental layout

```
                              ┌──────────────────────┐
                              │         LURA         │
                              │  (NE of Tyriova)     │
                              └──────────┬───────────┘
                    Welhyre pass + Trevelc↓ (flows SW)
                    ╔═══ LAGOON ══════╗
                    ║ separates Lura  ║   ┌────────────────────┐
                    ║ southern coast  ║   │      TYRIOVA       │
                    ║ from Gavonchaxo ║   │  (large empire,    │
                    ║ (cliffs + sea)  ║   │   cap. Treves 5M)  │
                    ╚═════════════════╝   └──────────┬─────────┘
                                        Trevelc → S  │  east of Tyriova
                                         ┌───────────▼─────────┐
                                         │    GAVONCHAXO       │
                                         │  (fairy forests,    │
                                         │   E of Tyriova)     │
                                         └──────────┬──────────┘
                                                    │ further east
                                         ┌──────────▼──────────┐
                                         │       XERVUR        │
                                         │  (desert wasteland; │
                                         │   only via Tyriova) │
                                         └─────────────────────┘
```

**Key facts:**
- Lura is **northeast** of Tyriova
- Gavonchaxo is **east** of Tyriova (not south); separated from Lura by a lagoon along the southern Naevyre coast
- Xervur is east of Tyriova/Gavonchaxo — only reachable through Tyriova
- The Trevelc valley (Welhyre pass) is Lura's sole practical exit to the world
- All other kingdoms reached by exiting via Welhyre and going southwest through Tyriova

## Lura — Internal Geography

### Shape and orientation
Enclosed oval, wider east–west than north–south. Four named mountain ranges form the walls.

## The Four Mountain Ranges

### Trevyre [tref-ire] — Northern Range
The highest and largest range in Lura. Forests (**Trechaxo**) line its southern foothills. Ancient, largely uninhabited. The destination dragons have been retreating to as Tyriova advances — many moved here from the Naevyre and other parts of the continent.

**Story role:**
- Pre-Xarra: optional side content; Trechaxo foothills and forest exploration; Yatuyas is here
- Post-Xarra: protagonist takes refuge here after Xarra's destruction and Lura's near-fall — wild survival, no fixed settlement

**Dragon connection:** The Trevyre is where dragons *go*. As Tyriovan pressure pushed them out of lower ranges and other territories, the Trevyre's height and remoteness made it the last refuge. Active dragon presence (in contrast to the Naevyre's abandoned feel), though still rarely encountered by humans.

**Named peaks:** TBD — dragon-connected; see Trevyre/Naevyre peaks section below.

### Xaryre [xar-ire] — Eastern Range
Runs north–south. Contains the mountain passes that are the strategic heart of the kingdom. Three major fortified cities control those passes; Royas sits in a valley on the eastern side of the range.

#### Pass settlements (north → south)

| Location | Pop. | Pronunciation | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hydea** | 300,000 | [hü-dea] | Hyre + Orde = "Mountain-Range Gravel" | Northern pass fortress; twin to Hydis. Forestry/sawmill economy outside the walls. The Breswelc runs alongside the mountain road into town. Heavy central fortification. SP main hub after Royas. |
| **Xarra** | 200,000 | [ksa-ra] | Xarv + Myra = "Dragon Canyon" | Capital of Lura and the oldest city in the region. Canyon city on plateau between Tresa and Roruk. Only reachable via Plateau Pass (from north/Hydea) or Vari Pass (from south/Hydis). Built in terraces up the mountain sides. Founded as the symbol of the covenant between dragons and the early humanoid peoples — built together, with dragons physically shaping the canyon stone. The oldest structures carry dragon-craft work no humanoid hand could have made alone. Its destruction in the SP story is the destruction of a civilisational symbol going back further than the Tyriovan Empire itself. |
| **Hydis** | 250,000 | [hü-dis] | Hyre + Oredis = "Mountain-Range Sand" | Southern pass fortress; twin to Hydea. Guards the southern exit of Vari Pass. |

#### Valley settlements (east side of Xaryre)

| Location | Pop. | Pronunciation | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Royas** | 10 | [ro-jas] | Hyro + Yas = "Mountain Village" | Valley east of the passes, northern half. Last place in Lura where humans and dragons coexist. Home of Takuya and adoptive father Taro Vaeres (master smith). **Character story start.** |
| **Gerlyas** | small | [ger-jas] | Grelu + Yas = "Valley Village" | Mid-to-southern valley, south of Royas. Isolated, traditional; carries the old dragon memory — no living dragons remain here, only the stories. More explicitly dragon-reverent than Royas, which simply coexists. |

#### Royas → Hydea connection
A smaller local trail/pass crosses the northern end of the Xaryre — not the main Plateau or Vari passes. First player journey (3–5 rooms), sets the mountain-crossing tone. Named **Nuvmito** [nuf-mito] (Myriac: Nuv + Mito = "Small/Deep Path").

#### Eastern valley road
**Grelmito** [grel-mito] — Myriac: Grelu + Mito = "Valley Path." Ancient rough track running north-south along the eastern face of the Xaryre; connects Royas, Gerlyas, and the approaches to Hydea/Hydis. Fits the Myriac-named path category (like the Nuvmito) rather than the Lurian road system — too old and informal to carry a Lurian name.

#### Named geographic features (Xaryre)
- **Tresa** [tresa] (Trev + Bresa) — western peak flanking Xarra's canyon
- **Roruk** [roruk] (Hyro + Paruk) — eastern peak flanking Xarra's canyon
- **Plateau Pass** — northern route into Xarra from Hydea area
- **Vari Pass** — southern route through the canyon from Hydis area
- **Whispering Woods** — forest in an eastern valley between Hydea and the passes
- **Forest Ralune** — ancient forest near Royas, eastern valley side; contains the **Lichtung der Ahnen** (Ancestors' Clearing), a spiritual site connecting present to past; quest-gated

### Welhyre [wel-hire] — Western Range
The Trevelc carved a valley through this range — Lura's only well-traversable exit to the world. Tyriova lies directly west. Several Luran forests (**Welchaxo**, **Evychaxo**) are in the western half of the country near this range.

**The pass:** The geographic pass is named **Melmito** [mel-mito] in Myriac — *Meleos + Mito* = "Connecting Path." The road built through it is the **Torumino** [to-ru-mi-no] in Lurian — "The Great Road." Two names, one place: the ancient mountain gap has a Myriac name; the road itself a Lurian one.

**Dragon history:** Traces of the extinct dragon community once living in this range survive — scattered carvings, foundation outlines, collapsed stone. No standing ruins; nothing dramatic. By the time of the game it is down to visible traces only. The Naevyre communities (especially Myrnev) still know of this place; the rest of Lura has forgotten.

**Open questions:**
- Named border town / waypost on Lura's western side of the pass?

### Naevyre [nef-ire] — Southern Range
Separates Lura from the sea to the south. The southern face drops into dramatic coastal cliffs. Second-to-last in average height across Lura's four ranges; has the least tall highest peaks of any range — relatively flat-topped ridges above sheer cliff drops.

**Character:** Remote, melancholic, half-forgotten. The communities here are economically vital to the kingdom (mining output) but feel abandoned by it. They carry traditions and histories the rest of Lura has forgotten — about the sea, about dragons, and about the time before Tyriova began advancing. Some dragons still live in the Naevyre peaks but haven't been seen in years; most retreated north to the Trevyre as Tyriovan pressure grew. The place feels emptied out even though it isn't.

**Dragon connection:** The Naevyre was once more actively inhabited by dragons. As Tyriova's army advanced, many left for the Trevyre. The local communities remember this — the dragon carvings in Myrnev's stonework, the names of the peaks, the old protocols for approaching a dragon's mountain. The rest of Lura has lost all of this.

**Contrast with Trevyre:** Naevyre = where dragon history *was*. Trevyre = where the dragons *went*.

#### Settlements

| Location | Pop. | Name meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Myrnev** | ~25,000 | Myra + Naev = "Dragon Sea-Cliff" | Main town; cliff-top administrative hub; dragon carvings in old stonework; sea visible from everywhere; small by Luran standards but the south's center |
| **Veryas** | ~300 | Veran + Yas = "Sea Village" | Fishing village in a cove break in the cliffs; boats lowered on ropes; most isolated; strongest old traditions |
| **Bresyas** | ~2,000 | Bres + Yas = "Stone Village" | Mining settlement inland on the mountain slopes; company-town character; supply caravans as only real link north |

**Named peaks:** TBD — dragon-connected; see Trevyre/Naevyre peaks section below.

**SP campaign:** Not relevant to the SP storyline.
**MP:** Story-relevant for multiplayer content (CO6+).

## Central Lura — The Lowlands

Flat plains and river systems. The population and economic heart of the kingdom.

### Rivers

| Name | Pronunciation | Meaning | Course |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Parwel** | [par-wel] | Paruk + Welc = "Snow River" | Flows from the Trevyre (northern range); feeds into Yavelca from the north |
| **Breswelc** | [bres-welk] | Bres + Welc = "Stone River" | Flows from the Xaryre (eastern range) through Hydea area; feeds into Yavelca from the east |
| **Trevelc** | [tre-felk] | Treva + Welc = "Great River" | Formed at Yavelca's fork where Parwel and Breswelc meet; flows west through Welhyre valley, then south through Tyriova to the sea |

### Forests

| Name | Pronunciation | Meaning | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Evychaxo** | [ewi-chaxo] | Evyna + Chaxo = "Red Forest" | Western Lura; Cayna's forest; the Rotwald |
| **Welchaxo** | [wel-chaxo] | Welca + Chaxo = "River Forest" | Western Lura along the Trevelc valley; near Welhyre foothills |
| **Trechaxo** | [tre-chaxo] | Treva + Chaxo = "Great Forest" | Southern foothills of Trevyre; large dense forest belt |

### Settlements

**Yavelca** (750,000) — [ja-wel-ka] — Yaves + Welca = "City of Rivers"
- Largest city in Lura
- Built on the fork where Parwel and Breswelc meet to form the Trevelc
- Economy driven by river trade
- Flat terrain; meadows and small forests surrounding
- Connections: road east to Hydea/Xaryre; road north toward Trevyre foothills; road west down Trevelc valley toward Welhyre and Tyriova
- **Multiplayer starting city** (pre-story era, CO6)
- In-game: 8–10 rooms; river districts, harbor on the fork, trade quarter, palace
- SP: falls midgame; Tyriovan army at its outskirts at story start

**Melaves** (~600,000) — [me-la-ves] — Meleos + Yaves = "Meeting City"
- Largest city on the Trevelc corridor after Yavelca; Lura's agricultural and commercial capital
- Famous for its grain markets, river docks, and market halls
- All major inland roads converge here — the place where Lura's harvests were weighed and shipped west
- SP: taken before story start; still partially inhabited but hollowed out — a city that died slowly; Tyriovan garrison present; empty market halls, looted grain towers; player can see what it once was from the bones of it
- MP: prosperous, bustling, the economic heart of the kingdom

**Caxaves** (~450,000) — [ca-xa-ves] — Chaxo + Yaves = "Forest City"
- Second major Trevelc corridor city; sits in the Welchaxo forest belt
- Economy built on lumber from the Welchaxo, river logistics, and westward trade staging
- More industrial and practical than Melaves — warehouses, mills, river staging yards
- SP: fully repurposed as a Tyriovan logistics hub; not destroyed but occupied — warehouses serve as military depots, lumber yards ship wood west; feels eerily functional rather than ruined
- MP: important but unglamorous trade and supply city

**Cayna** (30) — [kai-na] — Chaxo + Evyna = "Red Forest" (village named for its forest)
- Small village inside the Evychaxo
- Hometown of Mariko and Hayai
- Story significance: where the main party comes together

**Mitaves** (~400,000) — [mito-afwes] — Mito + Yaves = "Road City"
- Western gateway city; larger than Hydea
- Last major Luran city before the Welhyre pass and the Trevelc route to Tyriova
- Foreign merchants present; caravans stage here; economy built entirely around movement and trade
- SP: first major city to fall; taken before the story begins

**Gelyas** (small) — [gel-jas] — Gelu + Yas = "Gateway Village"
- At the western mouth of the Melmito pass; last Luran settlement before entering Tyriova
- New Myriac word: **Gelu** [ge-lu] = gate / mouth / opening
- Mixed population of customs guards, officials, and transient merchants; small permanent population (~5,000–10,000) but high foot traffic
- Defined entirely by what it sits at the end of — a border post, not a true city
- SP: fallen before even Mitaves; the first loss of the Tyriovan advance
- MP: lively, foreign-feeling; more outside influence here than anywhere else in Lura

**Breaves** (~80,000) — [bre-afwes] — Bres + Yaves = "Stone City"
- Trade crossing town on the Breswelc river between Hydea and Yavelca
- Named for the stone river it sits on; older than the surrounding plain towns
- Trio never visits; background world content

**Paraves** (mid-sized) — [pa-ra-ves] — Par + Yaves = "Snow-River City"
- Agricultural market hub on the Parwel, roughly midway between Yavelca and the Trechaxo belt
- Where the northern plains farmers bring grain; the economic center of the Parwel corridor
- SP: tense but not yet taken at story start — refugees from Yavelca flood north through here; the town is unsettled, unsure of its fate; the war has not arrived but its shadow has
- MP: quietly prosperous farming market town

**Trecyas** (small) — [trec-jas] — Trecha + Yas = "Great Forest Village"
- Last settlement before the Trechaxo; sits at the forest edge where the plains meet the foothills
- Forestry and hunting economy; inhabitants know the Trechaxo well
- The road into the forest toward Yatuyas begins here
- SP: eerily calm — the war hasn't reached this far north; the contrast with everything south feels unsettling
- MP: small and self-sufficient; a quiet forest-edge community

**Yatuyas** (small) — [jatu-jas] — Yatuve + Yas = "Wisdom Village"
- In the Trechaxo forest, southern Trevyre foothills
- Inhabitants keep old knowledge of the Lichtung der Ahnen (Ancestors' Clearing) in Forest Ralune
- Takuya passes through here and learns of the Clearing; story-significant
- Note: Forest Ralune is in or adjacent to the Trechaxo, not in the Xaryre valleys

**Brevas** (~25,000) — [bre-vas] — Brev + Yas = "Bridge Town"
- River crossing town on the Trevelc between Yavelca and Melaves; the most important crossing on the Werumino
- Economy: bridge tolls, river warehousing, trade staging
- SP: a choke point — one of the last crossing opportunities before the occupied cities; abandoned or fortified depending on when the player arrives
- MP: busy waypoint; river traffic constant

**Galuyas** (small) — [ga-lu-jas] — Galu + Yas = "Sky Village"
- Northern plains on a Parwel tributary, between Yavelca and Paraves
- Wide open farmland, big sky above the flat heartland; wheat and livestock

**Evyas** (small) — [ev-jas] — Evyna + Yas = "Red Village"
- Western plains near the edge of the Evychaxo; named for the red soil of the area
- Forestry and farming in equal measure; sits where the plains meet the forest fringe

**Gatoyas** (small) — [ga-to-jas] — Gaton + Yas = "Light Village"
- Central plains along the Burimino; known for its sunny open fields and long harvests
- Sits on a gentle rise in otherwise flat terrain — visible from far off

**Zalyas** (small) — [zal-jas] — Zalu + Yas = "People's Village"
- One of the oldest settlements in Lura; no special geography, no strategic value
- People have simply lived here longer than anyone can trace; old names, old customs, old stones

**Nuvyas** (small) — [nuv-jas] — Nuv + Yas = "Small Village"
- Supply waypoint on the southern road, roughly midway between Yavelca and the Naevyre foothills
- Practical and unglamorous — defined by where the road leads rather than what it is; farmers and caravan handlers
- SP: one of the safer places in the late game; Tyriovan advance comes from the west, not the south; likely a destination for refugees who fled the heartland; optional side content
- MP: a quiet agricultural waypoint on a road most people only use with purpose

## The Dragon Wars — History

All dates are relative to Year 0 = the start of the SP story (Takuya's timeline).

### The Pre-Imperial Kingdom (ancient — date TBD)

Before the Tyriovan Empire, the Lura region was home to an independent kingdom whose name is not yet established. This kingdom lived peacefully alongside the dragons — not as an exception, but as the foundation of its culture. The dragon-humanoid bond was not adopted from outside; it was original, the reason the kingdom existed in its form.

**Xarra** was already the capital. It was founded as the living symbol of the covenant between dragons and the other peoples of the region — built together, with dragons physically shaping the canyon stone alongside humanoid builders. The oldest structures in Xarra still carry dragon-craft work that no purely humanoid hand could have made. The canyon location was chosen deliberately: a threshold between the mountain world of the dragons and the lowland world of the humanoids — a place where both could meet as equals. The name *Xarra* (Xarv + Myra = "Dragon Canyon") preserves this founding truth even after everything that followed.

### Absorption into the Tyriovan Empire (~Year −1,800 onward)

The Tyriovan Empire was founded approximately 1,800 years before the SP story by a Myralu hero — itself a continuation of the same dragon-connected culture. The Lura region was gradually absorbed into the empire over generations with no conquest or formal moment; it grew into the empire, keeping its own identity and dragon culture throughout.

The pre-imperial kingdom has no remembered name — it predates written records and was absorbed so gradually that no single founding name survived. What survived instead was the name of the land itself: **Lura**.

**Etymology of "Lura":** Za[**lu**] + My[**ra**] — the second syllable of *Zalu* (human/people) and the second syllable of *Myra* (dragon/god) fused into a single word. "Lura" means, at its root, *People-Dragon* — the land defined by that bond. The name is older than the kingdom, older than the empire, possibly as old as the bond itself.

### The Myralu Decline (~Year −1,200 to −1,000)

Over 200 years, the Myralu imperial dynasty weakened — generational erosion of power, internal struggles, the dragon ties of the founding era slowly fading. A new non-Myralu faction rose within the empire.

### The First Dragon Wars (~Year −1,000 to −800)

**Trigger (~Year −1,000):** The new imperial family seized full control and immediately launched systematic persecution of Myralu and dragons — fearing the Myralu would reclaim power, they struck first.

**Phase 1 — The Hunting (~Years −1,000 to −900):** Tyriovan forces swept through all imperial territories hunting Myralu and dragon communities. Those who could flee moved into the Trevyre's deepest ranges. Dragon communities across lower territories abandoned their homes. Lura lost most of its openly Myralu population.

**~Year −900 — Vernmyr's Stand:** Tyriovan forces pushed through the Melmito pass into the Welhyre range to destroy the dragon community there. Vernmyr made his last stand defending them. Hyrogat — a human defender — died alongside him. The Welhyre dragon community was destroyed. The Naevyre communities witnessed and preserved this memory; the rest of Lura slowly forgot.

**Phase 2 — Imperial Strain (~Years −900 to −800):** Lura and Gavonchaxo drifted toward autonomy as the empire overextended. The Myralu appeared extinct — the primary goal seemed achieved. Dragons had vanished from Tyriova and everywhere outside Lura's Trevyre. Tyriova itself was near collapse.

**~Year −800 — The Wars End:** Tyriova declared partial victory and could not continue. Multiple factors converged: the Myralu appeared extinct; Lura's breakaway and Gavonchaxo's near-independence showed the cost of overextension; dragons had disappeared from Tyriovan territory; the empire was near collapse. Lura's independence was formally recognised (already a practical reality for decades). Gavonchaxo became a vassal state. Xervur remained semi-autonomous within the empire. The Trevyre dragons were beyond reach — Tyriova did not pursue into the highest northern range.

### The Long Stand (~Year −800 to 0)

Lura's independence did not mean peace. Tyriova never stopped pressuring the kingdom — 800 years of continuous political and military tension, enough to keep the memory of the first wars alive. Lura's identity crystallised entirely around being the last guardian of the dragons. The kingdom always understood that Tyriova had not given up, only paused.

**Zalmyr** — the celebrated Myralu hero who bridged the human and dragon worlds — belongs somewhere in this era (approximately Year −700 to −600). His story was once taught to schoolchildren across Lura. It is now largely forgotten outside the Trevyre region, suppressed or simply lost under 800 years of Tyriovan pressure. Whether he represents a Myralu who survived the first wars in secret, or a pre-war figure whose memory persisted into the independent kingdom's early age, is deliberately left ambiguous — tied to the larger question of whether any Myralu truly survived.

### The Second Dragon Wars (~Year 0 — the SP story)

The current Tyriovan monarch launches a full invasion of Lura. The motivation is primarily prestige: completing the historical mission the empire failed to finish 800 years ago. The dragons are too diminished to represent a genuine threat — this is not fear, it is legacy. Lura is the last obstacle; destroy Lura, destroy the last dragon refuge, close the chapter the first wars left open.

**The SP story is the Second Dragon War.**
